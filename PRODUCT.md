# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OfferMate serves two demo users: students reviewing roles inside one company, and HR viewers reviewing submitted candidates. Students need to compare roles quickly, understand why one role fits better than another, and see resume evidence without losing the original resume context. HR viewers need a concise view of submitted roles, candidate resume evidence, role fit, and interview follow-up points.

## Product Purpose

OfferMate demonstrates a job matching workflow built around resume parsing, JD parsing, explainable scoring, and two-sided review. Success means a viewer can understand the core workflow in a short walkthrough: parse a resume, inspect a company role pool, switch roles, read the score evidence, and review candidate fit from the HR side.

## Brand Personality

Clear, credible, and focused. The interface should feel like a practical recruiting tool, not a marketing page or a decorative concept demo. It can be warm and personal through the Davide branding, but task clarity is the priority.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized landing-page sections, generic card grids, decorative gradients, over-rounded panels, heavy glass effects, vague AI copy, and anything that makes the UI look generated rather than designed. Avoid wording that discusses how the demo was produced.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the role pool and the score explanation visually distinct.
2. Show evidence in place: JD evidence, resume evidence, and score details should connect to the exact item being discussed.
3. Use compact product UI density so the demo fits on laptop and mobile screens.
4. Prefer restrained state changes over decorative motion.
5. Keep HR language professional: assessment, evidence, screening, and interview follow-up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible focus states, stable layouts on mobile, and reduced-motion fallbacks. The demo should remain usable with keyboard navigation and should not rely on color alone to communicate match strength.
